Delete your Buddy account

Buddy by Raghav Patpatia (com.raghav.buddy_app)

A Buddy account exists only if you signed in with Google (for cloud backup or Buddy Arena). You can delete it and all of its cloud data in either of these ways.

In the app (takes effect immediately)

  1. Open Buddy and go to Settings.
  2. In the Cloud backup card, tap Delete account.
  3. Tap Delete to confirm.

Your account and its cloud data are deleted as soon as you confirm, and Buddy signs you out. You need an internet connection. If you are signed out, sign in with the same Google account first.

By email (if you can't use the app)

Email raghav.patpatia@gmail.com from the Google account you used to sign in to Buddy, with the subject "Buddy account deletion". We delete your account and its data within 30 days and confirm by email. If you write from a different address, we will ask you to confirm that the account is yours first.

What is deleted

  • Your Buddy account (the link to your Google sign-in)
  • Your cloud backup, including the data encrypted with your passphrase
  • Your Buddy Arena hunter card, hunter name and leaderboard entry
  • Your friends list and chats (for your friends too), friend requests, calls and parties
  • Online game rooms you hosted or joined, and the blocks you placed
  • Your family members' memories
  • Your money PIN record
  • Your phone's push notification token

Nothing from this list is kept afterwards.

What is not deleted

  • Data on your phone (money, health, medicines, settings and so on) stays until you uninstall Buddy or clear its storage in Android Settings. Songs in Download/Buddy and films in Movies/Buddy stay until you delete them in your Files app.
  • Analytics and crash reports are not linked to your account. Google deletes them automatically (crash reports after about 90 days, analytics data within 14 months).
  • A block that another player placed on you stays on their side. It holds only account IDs.
  • Data you sent to other services, such as a cloud AI provider you added your own key for, is covered by that service's own policy.
