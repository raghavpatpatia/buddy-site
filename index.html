Your buddy, on your phone.

Buddy is a free companion app for Android. You can talk to it, and it helps with your day: money, health, music, books, maps, travel, games and screen time. Most of it runs on your phone, including the AI, and much of it works offline.

  • On-device AI
  • Spend tracker
  • Medicines & health
  • Music & radio
  • Books & free films
  • Offline maps
  • Flights & trains
  • Reels counter
  • Phone cleaner
  • Buddy Arena games

Privacy first

Buddy reads your SMS, contacts and files only on your phone and never uploads them. The cloud backup is optional, and your money and health data are only ever uploaded encrypted with a passphrase that only you know. Buddy is for adults (18+).
