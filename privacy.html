Buddy Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026 · App: Buddy (com.raghav.buddy_app) for Android · Developer: Raghav Patpatia · Contact: raghav.patpatia@gmail.com

Buddy is a companion app for Android. You can chat with it, and it helps with money, health and medicines, music, books and films, maps and travel, screen time, phone cleaning and games with friends (Buddy Arena). This policy explains what data Buddy uses, where that data goes, and the choices you have.

The short version. Most of what Buddy does happens on your phone. Buddy reads your SMS, contacts and files only on the phone and never uploads them. Data leaves your phone only for the features listed below, and only the part that each feature needs. Buddy does not sell your data. Signing in, cloud backup, cloud AI and Buddy Arena are all optional.

1. Your account, cloud backup and Buddy Arena (Firebase)

Buddy uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions and Cloud Messaging), run by Google, to provide these optional features. The data is stored in Buddy's Firebase project.

Sign-in (optional)

You can sign in with your Google account. Firebase Authentication receives your name, email address and Google account ID, and gives you a Buddy user ID.

Cloud backup (optional, only when signed in)

  • Buddy backs up its settings and data automatically, shortly after something changes. It is stored under your user ID, and the database rules allow only your signed-in account to read or write it. Only the latest copy is kept.
  • Encrypted with your passphrase: your money data (transactions, balances, deposits, holdings and budgets), health data (meals, workouts, weight, step history, medicines and the doses you took), your profile and birth details, your chat with Buddy and its notes, SOS contacts and note, saved and nearby places, your job profile, your email address, reading progress and highlights, and saved passwords and keys (your email app password and any API keys you added). These are encrypted on your phone (PBKDF2-SHA256 and AES-256-GCM) with a passphrase that only you know, before they are uploaded. Without the passphrase nobody can read them, including the developer. If you have not set a passphrase, they are not uploaded at all.
  • Not encrypted with your passphrase: other settings, such as your theme, avatar look, to-do list, levels, XP and trophies, music favourites and saved stations, saved sites in Browse, and app preferences. They are protected by the access rules and Google's encryption at rest, so only your account can read them through the app.
  • Downloaded files (AI models, offline maps, saved books and songs) are not backed up.

Buddy Arena (optional, only when signed in)

  • Hunter card: your hunter name, avatar gender, weapon, level, lifetime XP, best wave and boss wins. Other signed-in players can see it on leaderboards and in search.
  • Friends and chat: friend requests, your friends list, chat messages and read markers. Only you and the friend involved can read them. When you send a message, Cloud Functions passes the sender's name and up to 300 characters of the text to Firebase Cloud Messaging, which delivers a notification to your friend.
  • Voice calls and parties: calls and Boss Arena parties go directly between phones (peer to peer, encrypted by WebRTC). To connect, the phones swap network addresses through Firestore and Google's public STUN servers. Call audio is not recorded or stored.
  • Online games (such as chess, tic-tac-toe, carrom and Quoridor): a game room with its code, the players and their moves. Only the players in a room can read it. Public rooms can be found through Quick match.
  • Blocks and limits: who you blocked, and a timestamp that limits how often requests can be sent.
  • Push token: a token for your phone, so calls and messages can reach you when Buddy is closed.

Family memories

From a certain level, a Buddy character can become your family member and remember the times you spent together. These memories are short notes, up to 280 characters each, based on your chats with that character. They are stored only on Buddy's server. The database rules stop any app or account from reading or changing them directly; only Buddy's server code can add them or send them back to your app. They are not encrypted with your passphrase. They are removed when you delete your account.

Money PIN

If you set a PIN to hide your money screens, a salted and peppered hash of the PIN is stored on Buddy's server in encrypted form. The PIN itself is never stored. If you forget it, a 6-digit code is emailed to your Google account's address through the Brevo email service. The code expires after 10 minutes.

App Check

Buddy uses Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity to check that requests come from the real Buddy app. Google receives information about the app and the device's integrity for this check.

2. Analytics, crash reports and performance

  • Firebase Analytics records how Buddy is used: which screens and features are opened, how long the app and AI replies take, the length of a message in characters, the phone's memory size, settings such as which AI model is on and whether the wake word is on, and the name of a radio station you play. It never records what you say, type, read or browse. Google Analytics also collects standard data such as an app-instance ID, the device model, the Android version and your approximate location from your IP address.
  • Firebase Crashlytics receives crash reports: the error and stack trace, device model, Android version, app version and memory use. Buddy also reports when Android closed the app itself (for example, when the phone ran out of memory).
  • Firebase Performance Monitoring measures timings such as how long the app takes to start.

This data is not linked to your name or email, and it is used only to fix bugs and improve Buddy. Google keeps it under Firebase's retention limits (Crashlytics reports for about 90 days, Analytics data for up to 14 months).

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

Buddy Arena can offer a rewarded video ad, for example to revive after you fall in a run. Watching one is your choice. Google AdMob serves these ads and may use your device's advertising ID, IP address and basic device information to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalised ads in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). See how Google uses information from apps that use its ad services. Buddy never gives AdMob your money, health, SMS, contacts, files or chat data.

6. Buddy's AI

  • On-device AI. Buddy can download an AI model (such as Gemma or Qwen) and run it on your phone. Whatever you send it stays on the phone. Buddy's voice (Kitten TTS) and handwriting recognition also run on the phone.
  • Cloud AI with your own key (optional). If you add your own API key for Google Gemini, OpenRouter or Amazon Bedrock (Anthropic Claude or Amazon Nova), the text for that request goes to that provider: your message, the recent conversation, facts Buddy remembers about you, and passages from documents you added to Buddy's library when they answer your question. Some features also use it: to-do sorting (the task text), food estimates (the food name), spending categories (merchant names only, never amounts, dates, balances or accounts), email drafts (the email you are replying to), job tips (your job profile), and the investing coach and stock analysis (the funds and stocks you hold and your risk preference). Your spending figures, SMS, health data and medicines are never sent to a cloud AI. The provider's privacy policy applies. Without a key, nothing is sent and the on-device model is used.

7. The 3D avatar

Buddy's 3D characters are drawn on your phone from models built into the app. Your choices (character, face, hair, outfit and colours) are saved on the phone and in your backup. One character's face is modelled on a real person, with that person's consent. No camera is used, and no photo of you is taken or uploaded.

8. Children

Buddy is for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has used Buddy, email us and we will delete their cloud data.

9. Sharing and selling

Buddy does not sell your personal data and does not share it, except with the services named in this policy, which process it only to provide their feature. Data may also be disclosed if the law requires it.

10. Security

Data sent over the internet uses encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Firestore rules limit who can read each piece of cloud data. Money, health and other personal data, and saved passwords and keys, are encrypted with your passphrase before upload. On the phone, passwords and keys are kept in Android's secure storage. Android's own cloud backup and phone-to-phone transfer are turned off for Buddy's data.

11. How long data is kept

  • On your phone: until you delete it in the app, clear Buddy's storage or uninstall Buddy. Songs you record and films you save stay in your Download/Buddy and Movies/Buddy folders until you delete them.
  • Cloud backup, Arena data, family memories and money PIN: until you delete them or delete your account. Each new backup replaces the previous one.
  • Analytics and crash reports: see section 2.
  • Other services: under their own policies.

12. Your choices and rights

  • Use Buddy without signing in. Nothing is then stored in Buddy's cloud.
  • Turn off any permission, or any part of Buddy, in Settings → My components.
  • See, correct or delete your data in the app. You can also restore your backup or sign out at any time.
  • Delete your account and all of its cloud data in the app, or by email. See how to delete your account.
  • Ask us for a copy of your cloud data, or ask any question about your data, at raghav.patpatia@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

13. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date. Important changes will also be shown in the app.
